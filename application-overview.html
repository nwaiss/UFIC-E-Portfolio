Application Overview

Our team developed an AI-powered chatbot for the Princess Vlei Forum, a community organization dedicated to the conservation and restoration of the Princess Vlei wetland in Cape Town, South Africa. The chatbot was designed to give visitors and community members easy, accessible information about the vlei's history, ecological significance, and the forum's ongoing conservation efforts. Built on a ChatGPT mini language model, the application prioritized simplicity and low cost so that it could remain a practical long-term tool for the forum without placing a financial burden on the organization.
